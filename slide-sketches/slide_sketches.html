01 · The Business Problem

Hardcoded logic is
killing scalability.

CORE ISSUE

Static, Code-Bound Configurations

The current Camera AI application relies heavily on hardcoded parameters embedded directly in the source code. Every customer change requires a code edit, rebuild, and redeploy.

BUSINESS IMPACT

Cost, Delay, Fragility

Adapting to new factory lines means developer intervention. Long-term support costs climb, customer onboarding slows, and the system becomes difficult to scale across Astra Otoparts sites.

02 · Proposed Solution

A configurable platform,
not a hardcoded script.

Transform Camera AI into a flexible system that adapts to every customer without touching Python.

OBJECTIVE 01

Configure Without Code

Workflows, detection rules, and thresholds become editable parameters — driven by a JSON template and a clean UI.

OBJECTIVE 02

Reduce Developer Dependency

Factory engineers and operators can adjust behavior themselves, freeing the engineering team to build — not babysit.

06 · Technical Challenge

Streaming video to AI
is heavy.

THE GAP

Continuous Streams Overload the Backend

Sending raw, continuous video to the AI inference service creates bandwidth bottlenecks, latency spikes, and unstable connections on factory networks.

THE SOLUTION

Canvas-Based Frame Capture

A hidden HTML5 <canvas> on the frontend extracts frames as Blobs, which are posted directly to FastAPI's UploadFile endpoint — lightweight, fast, stable.

HTML5 Canvas
Blob → UploadFile
Low Latency
Factory-Network Friendly
